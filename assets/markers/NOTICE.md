# Marker assets and trademarks

`r2d2.png` and `bb8.png` are the reference images used by the ORB marker
detector. They depict R2-D2 and BB-8, characters and trademarks of
Lucasfilm Ltd. / The Walt Disney Company. They are included only as small
fiducial markers in a non-commercial university robotics project and do not
imply affiliation or endorsement.

`assets/samples/markers_test.jpg` is a photograph of those markers printed on
paper, captured by the project authors for testing the detector.

If you reuse this repository, replace the marker images with your own
fiducials (AprilTags, ArUco boards, or original artwork) to avoid trademark
concerns.
